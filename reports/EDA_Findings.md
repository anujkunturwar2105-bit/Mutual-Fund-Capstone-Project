# Mutual Fund Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) - Key Findings

## Project Overview

This exploratory data analysis examines mutual fund industry data including NAV history, Assets Under Management (AUM), SIP inflows, investor demographics, geographic distribution, folio growth, scheme performance, risk-return characteristics, and portfolio holdings.

---

## 1. Mutual Fund Schemes

A total of **40 mutual fund schemes** were analyzed.

The dataset contains schemes from multiple fund houses and categories, providing a broad view of the mutual fund industry.

---

## 2. NAV Analysis

The average Net Asset Value (NAV) across the available NAV history was approximately **₹269.57**.

The highest NAV observed was **₹4,268.55**, corresponding to **AMFI Code 120844**.

NAV trend analysis was performed to understand historical movement across mutual fund schemes.

---

## 3. Assets Under Management (AUM)

**SBI Mutual Fund** had the highest AUM in the latest available period.

Its recorded AUM was approximately **₹1,250,000 crore**.

AUM analysis helps identify the largest asset management companies in the dataset.

---

## 4. SIP Investment Trend

The latest monthly SIP inflow recorded in the dataset was approximately **₹31,002 crore**.

The SIP time-series analysis indicates strong systematic investment activity and provides insight into retail investor participation.

---

## 5. Investor Demographics

The **26-35 age group** represented the largest investor segment.

This age group contained approximately **2,033 unique investors**.

Age-group, gender, income and city-tier analysis were used to understand investor characteristics and investment behaviour.

---

## 6. Geographic Distribution

**Punjab** recorded the highest investor participation in the transaction dataset, with approximately **441 unique investors**.

State-wise and city-wise analyses were performed to understand the geographic distribution of mutual fund investors.

---

## 7. Mutual Fund Folio Growth

Mutual fund folio accounts showed strong growth during the available analysis period.

Overall folio growth was approximately **96.98%**.

This indicates substantial expansion in mutual fund investor participation over the period represented by the dataset.

---

## 8. Scheme Performance

The best-performing scheme based on 3-year returns was:

**SBI Small Cap Fund - Regular Plan - Growth**

Its 3-year return was approximately **23.39%**.

Scheme performance was also evaluated using 1-year and 5-year returns.

---

## 9. Risk and Correlation Analysis

Correlation analysis was performed across:

- 1-year returns
- 3-year returns
- 5-year returns
- Alpha
- Beta
- Sharpe Ratio
- Sortino Ratio
- Standard Deviation
- Maximum Drawdown
- Expense Ratio

The analysis helps understand relationships between fund performance and risk characteristics.

Risk-return analysis was also used to compare fund returns with annualized volatility.

---

## 10. Portfolio Sector Allocation

**Banking** was the dominant portfolio sector across the analyzed mutual fund schemes.

Its average portfolio weight was approximately **21.74%**.

Sector allocation, individual stock holdings, market value, portfolio concentration and widely held stocks were analyzed.

# Conclusion

The exploratory data analysis demonstrates strong growth in mutual fund participation across SIP investments, investor folios and different investor segments.

SBI Mutual Fund emerged as the largest fund house by AUM in the available dataset, while SBI Small Cap Fund - Regular Plan - Growth recorded the highest 3-year return among the analyzed schemes.

The 26-35 age group represented the largest investor segment, while Punjab recorded the highest investor participation in the transaction dataset.

Banking was the dominant sector in mutual fund portfolio holdings.

Overall, the EDA provides a structured understanding of mutual fund industry trends, investor behaviour, fund performance, risk characteristics and portfolio allocation. These insights can be used as the foundation for further statistical analysis, machine learning and interactive dashboard development.
